Evaluation methods overview

Page 2 of 4 · Choosing a method

What do you need to learn?

A heuristic review helps identify likely usability issues. A cognitive walkthrough examines the steps a new user must take. Task-based testing shows how participants use the design.

Plan around a realistic task

Choose an activity that matters to your users. Define what you will observe and how the findings will inform the next iteration.

Distinguish a predicted usability issue from an issue observed during a participant session.
